# Apply packet prompt

Paste this when you are ready to apply to one or more queued roles. In the same message, paste your saved `PROFILE.md`, `FILTERS.md`, `RUBRIC.md`, and `VOICE.md`, the queue row or job link, and your resume if the assistant does not already have it.

---

You are preparing a paste-ready application packet for me. I will submit it myself. Never apply, never create an account, and never send a message for me. Never invent facts, metrics, tools, employers, or contacts.

If my profile or voice files are missing, stop and ask me to paste them.

## Live form scan

Open the public apply form and map every field in the exact order it appears: label, required or optional, type, and options. Note whether there is a cover letter or "additional information" field, and any uploads. If the form is behind a login, say so, prep from the posting plus any fields you can see, and mark the packet as needing a live pass once I can open the form.

## Packet to write

Give me a complete markdown packet I can save, with a short ID, containing:

1. **Basic info:** company, title, score breakdown if we have one, posting date, location, salary if posted, apply URL, one-sentence summary.
2. **Application (form order):** one section per field, in live form order. Each open-ended or unique field gets a paste-ready answer I can copy. Decision fields (work authorization, start date, salary expectation, referral, and the like) get a recommended answer from my files, flagged if I still need to confirm a number.
3. **Cover letter only if the form has a cover field** (required or optional). If there is no cover field, omit the letter.
4. **Packet notes:** form-order source, filter or location notes, anything unconfirmed.
5. **LinkedIn outreach** as the last section.

Write every long answer and any cover fresh for this company and role. Do not clone a prior packet and swap the name.

## Voice and facts

- Follow my `VOICE.md`. Sound like me, not like a generic assistant.
- No slang unless my samples use it. No consultant or AI filler.
- Never use em dashes, en dashes, or double hyphens. Use commas, periods, or parentheses.
- Honor my banned-word list, including: passionate, leverage, I'm thrilled, I'm excited to, synergy, spearhead, drive impact, at the intersection of, I'd love the opportunity, deeply, seamless, robust, unlock, empower, journey, resonate, not just X but Y, rhetorical questions, and lists of three adjectives.
- Use only facts in my profile, resume, or something I confirmed. Never invent, and never use anything on my do-not-claim list.

## Cover letter defaults

Unless my voice file says otherwise: about 320 to 360 words, 4 paragraphs, one Letter page, block format (no indents, one blank line between paragraphs, each paragraph one line in the source). Header from my profile only (do not invent contact details). Greeting `Hi <Company> team,` unless a verified person is the better addressee. Paragraph 1: the job and why I want it, with one specific true thing about the company. Paragraph 2: what I do now, confirmed facts only. Paragraph 3: two or three real pieces of work in concrete detail. Paragraph 4: an honest gap or stretch plus a true supporting detail, then a simple close.

## LinkedIn outreach

- **Send to:** a real, verified person plus a backup, with links: `Name, Title at Company (LinkedIn URL). Backup: Name, Title (LinkedIn URL).` Find them on public LinkedIn, the job post, or a company team page. Confirm they currently work there in a relevant role. Never invent a person. If the pick is inferred, mark it `(best guess)`. Use their first name. Do not leave a `[Name]` placeholder.
- Roles under 23: connect note (300 characters max) and a follow-up after they accept (about 500 to 700 characters).
- Roles scored 23 to 25: InMail if I use InMail (subject under 60 characters, body about 120 to 180 words). Otherwise use the connect note and follow-up.
- True facts only, my voice, no dashes, no markdown inside the message text.

## Voice check and ready bar

Before you show me a final draft, check it against my voice file: banned words, dash marks, invented facts, and whether it sounds like me. Fix issues first.

The packet is ready only if: the form was scanned or the login-wall limit is clearly marked; every unique question has a paste-ready answer; a cover is included only when the form has a field and that cover meets the rules; decision fields are filled or explicitly waiting on me; LinkedIn outreach is last and has verified or clearly labeled best-guess contacts; location and filters are honest; the role still meets my floor; and nothing on the do-not-claim list appears. If something is missing, say what is blocked. Do not hand me a stub as if it were done.

After I tell you I applied, give me an updated queue row and a single `applications.csv` line I can append: date, company, role, id, link, score, status, follow_up_date, notes.
