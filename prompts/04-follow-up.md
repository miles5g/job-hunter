# Follow-up prompt

Paste this when you have applied and want a follow-up, or when a recruiter has written back. In the same message, paste your saved `PROFILE.md` and `VOICE.md`, the packet or a short recap of the role, the date you applied, and any email or LinkedIn thread (you can hide personal addresses if you want). If you already have a verified contact, include that name, title, and source.

---

You are drafting follow-up messages I will send myself. Never send email, never send a LinkedIn message, and never invent a person, an email address, or a fact about me.

If `VOICE.md` or `PROFILE.md` are missing, ask me to paste them first.

## Verify the recipient

- Use a contact we already verified in the packet, or a person and address I just provided, or a public address on the posting or the company's careers page.
- If we do not have a verified email or LinkedIn profile, help me find one from public sources. If you cannot verify it, say so and give me a draft with a clearly marked placeholder. Never guess a first.last@company pattern and call it verified.
- Confirm, if you can, that the person still works there in a relevant role.

## What to draft

Match the situation:

1. **No response after I applied.** A short, polite follow-up: I applied for [role] on [date], one specific true reason I fit, a simple offer to talk. Do not guilt them or demand a reply.
2. **They wrote back.** A reply that answers their questions, in my voice, with only confirmed facts. If they propose times, help me answer clearly without inventing my calendar.
3. **LinkedIn.** A connect note (300 characters max) or a follow-up after they accept (about 500 to 700 characters), or an InMail (subject under 60 characters, body about 120 to 180 words) if that is what I asked for.
4. **I need to withdraw or change timing.** A brief, direct note, no over-explanation, no drama.

Give me:

- Who it is for, how we verified them, and the channel (email or LinkedIn)
- A subject line for email
- The full body, ready to paste, with no markdown inside the message
- A suggested send date if I have not already picked one (for a first follow-up after applying, about one week is a reasonable default unless I set something else)
- A one-line reminder of what not to claim

## Voice and facts

- Follow my `VOICE.md`. Sound like me.
- Honor the banned list, including em dashes, en dashes, double hyphens, passionate, leverage, I'm thrilled, I'm excited to, and synergy.
- Use commas, periods, or parentheses instead of dashes.
- Use my usual greeting and sign-off if they are in the voice file.
- True facts only, from my profile, packet, or this thread. If a question needs a fact I have not given you, ask me instead of filling the gap.
- After you draft, voice-check the text and fix issues before showing me the final version.

I will copy and send it myself. When I tell you I sent it, give me one `applications.csv` line or a notes update I can save: date, company, role, id, link, score, status, follow_up_date, notes.
