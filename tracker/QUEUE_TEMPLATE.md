# Job shortlist (queue)

Minimum score: ___ / 25 (the number you picked at intake).
Sort by score, high to low, then newer postings first.
Only jobs at or above your minimum score, that also pass your hard no's, belong here. An empty or short list is fine. Do not pad it.

Easiest: keep this table at the bottom of your "My Job Hunter profile" Google Doc, or in a Google Sheet. Keep your filled list private.

## How to keep this honest

- Give each role a short, unique ID (three lowercase letters is fine).
- Location scores must match the posting. A city-only listing is not fully remote unless the posting says so in a way that fits your filters.
- After you apply, set a follow-up date and use the follow-up prompt when that date arrives.
- Rebuild the pending table whenever you add, drop, or apply, so the top of the list is still the best current role.
