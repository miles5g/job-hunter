# Set up Job Hunter

Job Hunter helps you figure out what you want, find jobs worth your time, and write applications that sound like you. It never applies or sends anything for you. You stay in control of every submit and every message.

## Quick start (5 steps)

1. Open the [intake prompt](https://github.com/miles5g/job-hunter/blob/main/prompts/01-intake.md) and click the **copy** button (two overlapping squares) at the top right of the text.
2. Open a new chat in ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini. Paste the prompt, attach your resume if you have one, and answer the questions. It takes about an hour, and you can stop and come back.
3. At the end, the chat gives you five short profile files in one block. Copy all of it into one Google Doc (or a Notes note) called **My Job Hunter profile**. That is the only thing you need to save.
4. Whenever you want to find jobs, apply, or follow up, open a new chat, paste your **My Job Hunter profile** doc, then paste the matching prompt:
   - Find jobs: [hunt prompt](https://github.com/miles5g/job-hunter/blob/main/prompts/02-hunt.md)
   - Apply: [apply prompt](https://github.com/miles5g/job-hunter/blob/main/prompts/03-apply-packet.md) plus the application's questions
   - Follow up: [follow-up prompt](https://github.com/miles5g/job-hunter/blob/main/prompts/04-follow-up.md)
5. When you are ready to apply, install the free [Simplify Copilot](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/simplify-copilot-autofill/pbanhockgagggenencehbnadejlgchfc) Chrome extension to fill in the basic fields. Paste your custom answers into the rest, check everything, and press Submit yourself.

Want a bot that searches for new jobs every morning for you? Use Option A below instead.

The rest of this page has more detail on each step.

## Option A: Grok Bot (searches for you every morning)

1. Open the Job Hunter template link: https://x.ai/bot/1Bam7NFo7BJ98UjYreRB-
2. Click **Add to Grok Bot**. If you don't have the app yet, tap **Download** on that page first, make an account, then come back to the link and click **Add to Grok Bot**. Your copy is private to you.
3. You will see a note that the bot was made by another user and may act on your behalf. That note is standard for shared bots. This one never applies or sends anything without you.
4. Say hi. The bot starts the intake interview. When it asks for your resume, attach the file to your message (or paste the text). Answer one question at a time. Be honest about location, pay, dealbreakers, and how you actually write. It takes about an hour, and you can stop and pick up later.
5. At the end, the bot offers to connect your Gmail. Say yes if you want it to spot recruiter replies and interview requests and help draft follow-ups. It never sends email for you.
6. The bot asks: "Want me to search for new roles every morning and update your queue?" Say yes to turn that on. Your queue is your shortlist of jobs that score at or above your minimum score.
7. When you are ready to apply, ask it to prep a packet for a job on your shortlist. Copy the answers into the real application and submit it yourself. Follow-up drafts work the same way: copy, then send.

If the bot does not ask about Gmail or the morning search, just tell it: "connect my Gmail" or "set up a morning job search."

If anything in your profile looks wrong, tell the bot and have it fix it before you apply.

## Option B: Any AI chat (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini)

No download needed. Everything is on the [project page](https://github.com/miles5g/job-hunter).

### 1. Get a prompt

Open the prompt you need and click the **copy** button (two overlapping squares) at the top right of the text:

- [01 Intake](https://github.com/miles5g/job-hunter/blob/main/prompts/01-intake.md) (do this first, once)
- [02 Hunt](https://github.com/miles5g/job-hunter/blob/main/prompts/02-hunt.md) (find and score jobs)
- [03 Apply](https://github.com/miles5g/job-hunter/blob/main/prompts/03-apply-packet.md) (answers for one application)
- [04 Follow up](https://github.com/miles5g/job-hunter/blob/main/prompts/04-follow-up.md) (follow-up and reply drafts)

Copy the whole thing, including the short note at the top. The AI knows to skip it.

Optional: if you would rather have everything on your computer, click the green **Code** button on the project page, then **Download ZIP**. On Windows, right-click the downloaded file and choose **Extract All**. On a Mac, double-click it. The prompts are in the `prompts` folder. Open them with Notepad (Windows) or TextEdit (Mac).

### 2. Do the intake (about an hour)

Open a new chat, paste the intake prompt, and attach or paste your resume if you have one. Answer one question at a time. You can stop anytime and continue in the same chat later. If the chat cuts off, open a new chat, paste the intake prompt plus whatever profile text you already have, and say "continue intake."

No writing samples? That's fine. The chat will have you answer one question the way you would write to a hiring manager.

### 3. Save your profile in one Google Doc

At the end, the chat gives you five short profile files (Profile, Filters, Scoring, Voice, Targets) together in one block. Copy all of it into one Google Doc or Notes note called **My Job Hunter profile**. You do not need to make separate files. If you change something later, ask the chat for the full updated block and replace the old one.

### 4. Find jobs (whenever you want)

Open a new chat, paste your **My Job Hunter profile** doc, then paste the hunt prompt. Also paste your shortlist if you have one.

- If the chat can search the web, it looks for jobs and scores them.
- If it can't, search Indeed, LinkedIn, or your field's job sites yourself, and paste 3 to 5 job descriptions into the chat. Copy the text, not just the link (LinkedIn links need a login). It scores them for you.
- Always click a job link yourself before trusting it. AI chats sometimes make up listings.

Only jobs at or above your minimum score make your shortlist. An empty or short shortlist is fine. It means nothing great showed up yet.

Keep your shortlist at the bottom of your Google Doc, or in a Google Sheet.

### 5. Apply

Open the real application yourself. Copy its questions, or take screenshots of them. In a new chat, paste your profile doc, the job description or link, the questions or screenshots, and the apply prompt. You get paste-ready answers, plus a short cover letter only if the application asks for one.

Simplify fills in the basic fields (name, contact info, work history). Paste the custom answers into the rest. Check everything, then press Submit yourself.

### 6. Follow up

After you apply, or when a recruiter writes back, paste your profile doc, the follow-up prompt, and the email or message thread. Copy the draft and send it yourself.

### 7. Keep track (optional)

Keep a simple spreadsheet of where you applied. In Google Sheets, choose **File > Import > Upload** and pick `tracker/applications.csv` from the download, or just type these column headers into a new sheet: date, company, role, id, link, score, status, follow_up_date, notes.

## When you're ready to apply: Simplify

Install the free [Simplify Copilot](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/simplify-copilot-autofill/pbanhockgagggenencehbnadejlgchfc) Chrome extension. Make a free account and fill in your profile once (contact info, work history, education, resume). When you open an application, it fills in the standard fields.

- It works in Chrome on a computer, not on a phone.
- Skip Autopilot (it submits applications for you) and the paid upgrades. The free extension is all you need.
- You still check every answer and press Submit yourself.

## If you get stuck

- Hunts and applications need your profile. If the chat seems lost, paste your **My Job Hunter profile** doc again.
- Keep the shortlist honest. Don't lower your minimum score just to fill space.
- If a draft doesn't sound like you, point at the line and ask the chat to rewrite it using the Voice part of your profile.
- Your filled-in profile and resume are personal. Don't post them publicly.
