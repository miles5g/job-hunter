# How jobs get scored

(In the chat version this section is called SCORING.)

Score every job before it can go on the shortlist. Five dimensions, 1 to 5 points each, for a total of 25.

**Minimum score: ___ / 25** (set at intake; 22 if none was set). Career changers and new grads often start at 18 to 20. Jobs below the minimum score do not go on the shortlist. Do not lower it just to fill space. An empty or short shortlist is fine.

During intake, the assistant drafts the 5 / 4 / 3 / 2 / 1 descriptions so they match this person, and the person confirms or fixes them. Keep the five dimension names unless they clearly need different ones.

## Dimensions

### 1. Fit (does this job match what they can do and want to do?)

| Score | Criteria |
|-------|----------|
| 5 | Clear match to their target function, skills, and the work they want to spend time on |
| 4 | Strong match with a small stretch they can back up with real evidence |
| 3 | Adjacent. Readable, but not what they are aiming for |
| 2 | Mostly a mismatch (wrong function, wrong day-to-day, or requires a credential they do not have) |
| 1 | Hard miss (wrong field, or a title they already listed as a no) |

### 2. Level (is this the right seniority and pay band?)

| Score | Criteria |
|-------|----------|
| 5 | Seniority and posted pay clearly fit their band |
| 4 | Slight stretch or slight under, still realistic, pay is at or above the lowest pay they will take |
| 3 | No salary posted, but the level makes their lowest pay realistic |
| 2 | Likely under their lowest pay, or a big seniority mismatch |
| 1 | Far below their lowest pay, unpaid, commission-only when they need a salary, or a seat well below the level they are aiming for |

### 3. Location (does this actually work for their life?)

| Score | Criteria |
|-------|----------|
| 5 | Matches their best-case setup (often fully remote in an allowed country) |
| 4 | Allowed hybrid or onsite under their stated rules |
| 3 | Location is unclear. Do not treat this as a 5. Ask or research before applying |
| 2 | Allowed only with a real compromise (heavier office load, longer commute, limited travel) |
| 1 | Fails their location rules (wrong city with no qualifying remote option, required relocation they will not do) |

Be honest. A city name without an explicit remote policy that fits their filters is not a 5.

### 4. Growth (will they learn and move forward?)

| Score | Criteria |
|-------|----------|
| 5 | Clear room to build skill, scope, or career in the direction they want |
| 4 | Solid learning and ownership, even if the ladder is less formal |
| 3 | Fine, but mostly a lateral move |
| 2 | Stagnant, or a step backward they would regret |
| 1 | A dead end, or work that would shrink their options |

### 5. Joy (would they want this Tuesday?)

| Score | Criteria |
|-------|----------|
| 5 | The product, team, or craft is something they would be glad to talk about |
| 4 | Interesting work and a culture that fits how they like to operate |
| 3 | Neutral. Fine if the other scores are strong |
| 2 | Draining day-to-day, even if the logo is impressive |
| 1 | They would dread it (values clash, politics, or work they already want to leave behind) |

## How to score

- Score from the posting, the career page, and other public facts. If a fact is missing, say so. Do not invent it.
- If a hard filter in `FILTERS.md` fails, the role is out. Do not score it into the queue.
- Prefer roles that score 25 when you have a choice.
- When two roles tie, prefer the newer posting.
- Default freshness window: first posted in the last 21 days, unless the person sets a different window.

## Minimum score and exceptions

- Minimum score: the number set at intake (change only if the person sets a new one)
- Optional exception, only if they say yes to it during intake: a dream company or rare role may enter at a slightly lower total if Joy is at least 4, nothing is below 3, and no hard filter fails. If they did not say yes, there is no exception.
